# Fan Hub Plus — User Guide & Walkthrough

Welcome to **Fan Hub Plus**! This guide outlines the key features and user journeys available across the platform for Visitors and Registered Users.

---

## 1. Visitor Experience (Unauthenticated)

Visitors can explore public content without creating an account:

### 1.1. Homepage Portal (`/`)
- **Fandom Universe Categories**: Quick access to all 8 core fandom realms (Anime, Gaming, Movies, TV Shows, K-Pop, Comics, Manga, Cosplay).
- **Featured Universe Content**: Highlights trending lore, canonical articles, and breaking franchise news.
- **Release Radar**: Real-time countdowns for upcoming games, anime seasons, and movies.
- **Upcoming Fan Events**: Dates, venues, and external ticket links for upcoming conventions and expos.
- **Visual Sitemap**: Footer navigation providing direct links to all public sections.

### 1.2. Universe Content Explorer (`/explore`)
- Search by keyword across titles and descriptions.
- Filter simultaneously by **Category**, **Genre**, **Content Type** (Article, Guide, Canon Lore), and **Publication Status**.
- Click any entry to view full markdown lore breakdown, author credits, related media, and user reviews.

### 1.3. Character Profiles & Lore (`/characters`)
- View full character dossiers including biography, origin fandom, ability tags, and spotlight badges.

### 1.4. Multimedia Center (`/multimedia`)
- Filter official soundtracks, game trailers, anime clips, and fan artwork across all 8 fandoms.

### 1.5. Event Calendar & Map Discovery (`/events`, `/events/calendar`)
- Filter events by city, attendance format (in-person vs livestream), and date.
- Export event schedules to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook via `.ics` file downloads.

---

## 2. Registered User Experience (Authenticated)

Creating an account unlocks the full suite of interactive community features:

### 2.1. Account Registration & Authentication (`/register`, `/login`)
- Register with your name, email, and password.
- Secure session management with persistent "Remember Me" authentication.

### 2.2. Member Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
- Real-time overview of your personal bookmarks count, submitted fan creations, total reviews submitted, and chatbot sessions.
- Direct quick-actions to submit fan works, update profile, or explore favorite fandoms.
- Recent platform activity log tailored to your account.

### 2.3. Profile & Theme Preferences (`/profile`, `/profile/edit`)
- Update display name, bio, location, and social website links.
- Upload custom profile avatar image.
- Select your **Favorite Fandom Realms** for personalized recommendations.
- Configure persistent UI preferences:
  - **Theme Mode**: Seamless Dark Mode / Light Mode toggle.
  - **Accessibility**: High contrast mode, reduced animations, adjustable font sizes.

### 2.4. Bookmarking & Personal Annotations (`/bookmarks`)
- Click the bookmark icon on any content, event, release, or character across the platform.
- Add private personal notes to any bookmark (e.g. *"Catch up on episode 12 before convention"*).

### 2.5. 5-Star Ratings & Community Reviews
- Rate lore articles and canon entries from 1 to 5 stars.
- Real-time atomic computation updates the overall average score dynamically.

### 2.6. Fan Community Submissions (`/community/submit`)
- Share your creative fan works: Fan Art, Fan Fiction, Video Edits, Cosplay Showcase, or Theories.
- Upload cover images, embed external video links, and format your story in rich markdown.
- Track submission status in real-time (`Pending Review`, `Approved & Published`, `Rejected with Feedback`).

### 2.7. AI Smart Fan Assistant (`/chatbot`)
- Chat in real-time with the intelligent fandom assistant.
- Ask questions about lore, upcoming release dates, platform FAQs, or character abilities.
- Browse and continue previous chat history sessions.

### 2.8. Platform Feedback (`/feedback`)
- Send bug reports, feature suggestions, or queries directly to platform administrators.
